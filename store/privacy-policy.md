# FixIt — Privacy Policy (template — have counsel review before launch)

Last updated: 2026-10-02

FixIt ("we") collects as little data as possible.

## What we collect
- Photos and descriptions you explicitly submit for diagnosis (processed to
  return troubleshooting steps; not sold, not used for ads).
- Optional account data (email, Apple/Google ID) only if you sign in, used for
  cross-device sync, favorites, and purchase restoration.
- Purchase receipts, verified server-side with the store (Apple/Google/Microsoft).
- Minimal diagnostics (app opened, diagnosis requested, step completed) without
  unnecessary personal information.

## What we do NOT collect
- No advertising trackers. No sale of personal data.

## Your controls
- Delete saved projects and favorites in-app at any time.
- Delete your account and server data via Settings → Delete Account
  (also calls `DELETE /api/account`).
- Camera, microphone, photo-library, and location permissions are requested
  only for the feature that needs them and can be revoked in OS settings.

## AI processing
Images you submit may be processed by our AI service to identify the problem
and generate repair steps. Emergency or dangerous situations trigger a safety
message instead of repair instructions.

## Contact
Support: https://fixit.app/support
